# UV and visible absorption teaching data

All files are simulated, generated by `Rscript scripts/generate-data.R` from the
project root with seed 207. `Rscript R/analyse-uv-visible.R` derives outputs and
all analytical figures. No observed environmental measurements are included.
The spectral envelope is an illustrative Gaussian, not an authentic reference
spectrum. The linear response is a teaching model, not a published absorptivity.

## Analytical scenario and concentration basis

W1, W2 and W3 are supplied filtered, fortified water samples. Assume all dissolved
iron in each aliquot is quantitatively reduced to iron(II), complexed with excess
1,10-phenanthroline under controlled acidity, and retained in the final solution.
No digestion or recovery validation for particles is supplied. The intended
result is mg Fe/L in the supplied filtered water under these assumptions, not
particulate iron or a validated total-iron result for arbitrary matrices.

All standards, QC and samples undergo matched colour development before reading
at 510 nm in a 1.00 cm cell. Concentrations of standards and QC refer to **final
coloured solutions**, expressed as mass of elemental iron per litre. They are
not mg of the complex per litre. The reference is a reagent blank; absorbance
is already referenced to it. Do not subtract a second blank from these files.
A small residual offset is present; fit an intercept without assigning its cause.

These are analysis assumptions, not a bench protocol. Actual reagent quantities,
reaction time, sample preservation and waste handling would come from an approved
laboratory protocol.

## File dictionary

| File | One row represents | Columns and units |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `standards.csv` | An independently prepared final calibration solution, measured once | `standard_id`: unique solution; `standard_mg_l`: mg Fe/L final; `replicate`: 1–3 at each level; `absorbance`: dimensionless, 510 nm |
| `readings.csv` | One repeat reading of a prepared sample solution | `preparation_id`: preparation key; `reading_id`: 1–3 within preparation; `absorbance`: dimensionless, 510 nm |
| `preparations.csv` | One independent preparation from a supplied water sample | `preparation_id`: unique key; `sample_id`: collected sample; `block`: A or B; `aliquot_ml`, `final_ml`: mL; `u_aliquot_ml`, `u_final_ml`: supplied standard uncertainties in mL; `note`: preparation context |
| `qc.csv` | One reading of an independently sourced check solution for a measurement block | `block`; `reading_id`: 1–3; `reference_mg_l`: mg Fe/L in final solution; `absorbance`: dimensionless, 510 nm |
| `spectra.csv` | One wavelength position within one illustrative scan | `wavelength_nm`: nm; `trace`: reagent_blank or iron_standard; `absorbance`: dimensionless |

Every scan position belongs to the same solution; it is not another sample.
P01 and P02 come from W1. P03 and P04 come from W2: P04 uses a **new** 5.00 mL
aliquot after P03's 10.00 mL aliquot gave an out-of-range response. P05 belongs
to W3 in the later block B. The preparation table is deliberately in a different
order from the readings, so match by `preparation_id`.

Calibration applies to block A and is checked again before use in later block B.
An independent QC material at 2.00 mg Fe/L has a teaching acceptance window of
95–105% recovery. Block A passes; B fails. Withhold all block B sample results
until the cause is investigated and calibration and QC are satisfactory.
The failure does not retroactively invalidate the earlier accepted block A.
This window is supplied for the exercise; it is not a universal method criterion.

## Uncertainty model

Assume normal, independent response errors of constant variance, a correct linear
response from 0–4 mg Fe/L, negligible uncertainty in calibration concentrations,
and equal reading variance for standards and unknowns. The supplied volume
uncertainties are standard uncertainties and independent of response errors.
For the simple preparation budget, treat aliquot and final volume as independent.
QC reference uncertainty is neglected for this teaching check.

The budget excludes sampling, incomplete reduction, chemical recovery, matrix
bias, cell path variation, stock-standard uncertainty, and correlated volume
errors. The replicate preparations are not a full study of between-preparation
variance. Do not interpret the budget as full method validation.
