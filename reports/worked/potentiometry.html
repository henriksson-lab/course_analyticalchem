Chemical interpretation

All inputs are simulated teaching data. The fitted fluoride slope is −59.10 mV/decade; the accepted 121.2 mV sample gives 1.99915e−4 mol/L. The early check error is +0.12 mV and later error +6.02 mV; the later block fails the supplied ±2 mV criterion and its sample estimate is withheld.

Transfer and limits

A 17.8 mV decrease gives concentration factor about 2.001. Activity/concentration matching, temperature, junction and interferences matter. This free-ion-responsive procedure does not establish total fluorine across chemical forms.

The complete executable solution is in R/analyse-potentiometry.R. The student lesson and data dictionary specify the measurand, input units and row meanings. A conditional teaching result is not full method validation.
