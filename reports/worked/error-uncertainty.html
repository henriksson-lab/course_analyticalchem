Chemical interpretation

All inputs are simulated teaching data. A and B have the same reading SD, about 0.01581 mg/L; B has estimated bias +0.50 mg/L. C has no mean difference in this small series but much larger reading SD, 0.31623 mg/L. These properties must not be confused with the full method uncertainty.

Transfer and limits

The dilution is 10.00 mg/L with standard uncertainty 0.04219 mg/L from independent stock and volume inputs. Halving the stock uncertainty gives 0.02408 mg/L. C’s reading uncertainty in its mean is 0.14142 mg/L. Sampling, recovery and common biases remain outside those models.

The complete executable solution is in R/analyse-error-uncertainty.R. The student lesson and data dictionary specify the measurand, input units and row meanings. A conditional teaching result is not full method validation.
