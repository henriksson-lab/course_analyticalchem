Chemical interpretation

All inputs are simulated teaching data. Final-case independent QC recovers 100.667%. C1 supports 150 mg/L in drink A. C2 is outside range and its reported concentration is withheld. C3 is a new smaller aliquot of drink B and supports 400 mg/L. C4 is unresolved and withheld. Reasons remain attached to IDs in the output.

Transfer and limits

The supplied final standard uncertainties scale to 1.5 and 4.0 mg/L for C1/C3; they are not 95% intervals and exclude volumes, sampling and recovery. Transfer gives 200 mg/L if accepted. Concentration-dependent method differences motivate investigation but do not prove a chemical mechanism. A single nominal ion mass does not identify caffeine.

The complete executable solution is in R/analyse-defending-results.R. The student lesson and data dictionary specify the measurand, input units and row meanings. A conditional teaching result is not full method validation.
