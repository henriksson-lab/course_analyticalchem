Chemical interpretation

All inputs are simulated teaching data. Equal-mass strata average to 15.67 mg/kg. Top-only sampling averages about 8 mg/kg despite small within-position scatter. Extracted caffeine is 0.480, 0.490 and 0.750 mg/g for T1a, T1b and T2a. T1a/T1b are preparations of one material. These are extracted amounts, conditional on the supplied concentration and transfer model; total native recovery is not established.

Transfer and limits

The new preparation gives 1.50 mg/g. Ideal extraction recoveries are 81.8% for one 10 mL extraction and 90.5% for two 5 mL extractions. A post-extraction spike cannot establish extraction recovery.

The complete executable solution is in R/analyse-sampling-preparation.R. The student lesson and data dictionary specify the measurand, input units and row meanings. A conditional teaching result is not full method validation.
