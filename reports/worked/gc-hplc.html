Chemical interpretation

All inputs are simulated teaching data. External estimates for repeated injections I1/I2 of D1 are 15 and 12 mg/L final, while area-ratio estimates are 15.000 and 15.015 mg/L. Multiplying by the tenfold preparation factor gives approximately 150 mg/L original. These are repeated injections of one preparation. I3 is unresolved and I4 has failed QC; their reportable concentration fields are missing.

Transfer and limits

Transfer ratio 903/600 gives 14.99 mg/L final and 74.95 mg/L original. The fixed-IS-concentration calibration cannot simply transfer to samples with a different IS concentration. Internal standardisation cannot fix coelution, differential matrix effects or analyte loss before standard addition.

The complete executable solution is in R/analyse-gc-hplc.R. The student lesson and data dictionary specify the measurand, input units and row meanings. A conditional teaching result is not full method validation.
