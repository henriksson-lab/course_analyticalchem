Chemical interpretation

Transfer and limits

The transfer digest is 0.0800 × 5 − 0.0100 = 0.3900 mg/L; result 48.75 mg/kg dry mass. Elemental detection does not preserve molecular identity or original oxidation states. Recovery, mass, volume, calibration and blank variation need attention in a full uncertainty budget.

The complete executable solution is in R/analyse-atomic-methods.R. The student lesson and data dictionary specify the measurand, input units and row meanings. A conditional teaching result is not full method validation.
