Chemical interpretation

All inputs are simulated teaching data. The standard-addition x-intercept is −2.00802 mg/L in the final flask. Its magnitude multiplied by 25/10 gives 5.02005 mg/L original. The external-calibration answer is 3.76126 mg/L because the simulated sample matrix suppresses sensitivity. Incremental recovery is 75%.

Transfer and limits

The transfer result is 8.00 mg/L. Fixed sample fraction, equal final volume, linear additive response, appropriate background correction and equivalent native/spike behaviour are essential. A late spike cannot establish early extraction recovery. The negative-intercept estimate remains uncertain; no full interval is supplied.

The complete executable solution is in R/analyse-matrix-recovery.R. The student lesson and data dictionary specify the measurand, input units and row meanings. A conditional teaching result is not full method validation.
