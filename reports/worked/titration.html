Transfer and limits

Hardness results are 123.1107, 124.1116 and 191.1719 mg/L as CaCO3. The transfer is 160.144 mg/L as CaCO3. State blank subtraction and the conditional selective 1:1 Ca/Mg–EDTA model. Do not infer carbonate presence or separate calcium and magnesium concentrations.

The complete executable solution is in R/analyse-titration.R. The student lesson and data dictionary specify the measurand, input units and row meanings. A conditional teaching result is not full method validation.
