Chemical interpretation

All inputs are simulated teaching data. The retained-intercept line gives 2.39813 mg Fe/L final for the supplied unknown. The approximate inverse interval is 2.38857–2.40769 mg Fe/L under the stated linear, independent, constant-variance response model with negligible calibration-concentration uncertainty. A forward-response interval is not itself a concentration interval.

Transfer and limits

The separate indicative blank model gives 0.02023 and 0.06742 mg/L for the 3-SD and 10-SD increments, not universal validated limits. Transfer: 0.024 mg/L. Outside-range samples need appropriate new measurements; failed QC affects reportability; below-quantification results need an appropriately qualified report, not zero.

The complete executable solution is in R/analyse-calibration-qc.R. The student lesson and data dictionary specify the measurand, input units and row meanings. A conditional teaching result is not full method validation.
