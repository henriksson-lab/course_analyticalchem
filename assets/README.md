# Figure provenance

All SVG figures are original course material. No textbook figure or third-party
photograph has been copied. `complex.svg`, `light-path.svg`, `dilution.svg`,
`replicates.svg`, and `workflow.svg` are conceptual diagrams; their labels and
captions state what they represent. The complex drawing is not a molecular
structure or geometry model. `R/analyse-uv-visible.R` rebuilds `spectrum.svg`,
`calibration.svg`, `residual.svg`, `range.svg`, `qc.svg`, and `uncertainty.svg`
from the supplied simulated datasets. The light-path drawing represents apparatus
schematically; it is not a photograph of an actual instrument.


The remaining course uses original SVG diagrams in `assets/course/`, generated
by `scripts/generate-course-diagrams.py`, and analytical plots generated by
`R/analyse-<unit>.R`. Their simulated inputs and assumptions are documented in
`data/course/README.md`. Physical apparatus drawings are schematic concepts,
not photographs or exact engineering drawings. The conceptual atomic lines
and mass spectrum must not be used as identification references.
